🌍 Earth Overview

Our planet is the only known world to harbor life. Formed 4.5 billion years ago, Earth orbits the Sun at a distance of 150 million kilometers.

Age: 4.54 Billion Years

🌊 Oceans & Water

71% of Earth's surface is covered by water. The oceans contain 97% of Earth's water and support incredible biodiversity.

Volume: 1.335 Billion km³

🏔️ Mountains & Continents

Seven continents shape our world. The highest mountain is Mount Everest at 8,849 meters above sea level.

Continents: 7

🌤️ Atmosphere

Earth's atmosphere is composed mainly of nitrogen (78%) and oxygen (21%), protecting life from solar radiation.

Pressure: 101.3 kPa

🌙 The Moon

Earth's only natural satellite, the Moon is 384,400 km away. It causes tides and stabilizes Earth's rotation.

Orbital Period: 27.3 Days

🌱 Biodiversity

Earth hosts an estimated 8.7 million species. Rainforests, coral reefs, and ecosystems cover diverse habitats.

Species: 8.7 Million

⚡ Natural Resources

Rich in fossil fuels, minerals, and renewable energy sources. Sustainable management is crucial for future generations.

Renewable Focus: Growing

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Human Population

Over 8 billion people inhabit Earth, spread across countries and cultures. Earth is home to incredible human diversity.

Population: 8+ Billion

🌡️ Climate & Weather

Earth experiences diverse climates from tropical to polar. Climate change is reshaping our planet's future.

Avg Temp: 15°C

🚀 Space & Exploration

Humanity is exploring space from Earth's surface. Our satellite network and space programs expand our cosmic reach.

Active Satellites: 5,000+
